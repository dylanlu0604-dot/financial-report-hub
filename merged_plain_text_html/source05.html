Our survey, administered by
Guidepoint

-

about the future of private credit. The survey included a diverse group of financial professionals familiar with the
industry, with 36% representing general partners at alternative funds, 36% limited partners and fund investors, and

28% bankers and lenders. Portfolio/investment manager was the most common job title, representing 41% of
respondents, followed by lender, debt capital markets, banker (23%), fund allocator (7%) and limited partner (6%).
The group was balanced across geographies, with 35% based in Europe, 34% in Asia and 31% in North America.
